The Canvas of the People

Long before modern galleries, the walls of mud huts, temple scrolls, and handmade paper served as canvases for India's rural and tribal communities. Discover the vibrant folk and tribal painting traditions that capture the soul of the subcontinent.

Common Threads & Motifs

Nature & Cosmos

From Warli's cycle of life to Gond's intricate animal motifs, nature is not just a backdrop but the central subject of folk art.

Mythology & Rituals

Epics like the Ramayana and Mahabharata, along with local deities, are depicted in Pattachitra scrolls and Madhubani wall paintings.

Natural Pigments

Artists traditionally use colors derived from turmeric, indigo, lampblack, and crushed minerals, ensuring an eco-friendly creative process.

Women Artisans

In many traditions like Madhubani and Warli, the art form has been historically preserved and passed down exclusively by women.
